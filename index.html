Methodology & caveats

XRP transactions carry no geographic data. Locations shown here map known exchange and institutional wallets to the entity's home city; payments touching unidentified wallets are placed at a plausible city weighted by regional exchange activity and are marked estimated.

Holdings figures combine Ripple's published escrow balance with public rich-list and exchange-reserve snapshots. Exchange balances move daily; treat these as order-of-magnitude estimates, not audited figures. "Self-custody & unidentified" is the remainder of total supply and has no reliable geography.
