Highlights — what matters right now

  • Flows just turned: +$39.78M the week of Aug 17–21 — the best week since May, after July nearly flatlined at +$27.29M with zero-flow days on half its sessions.
  • The core tension: Ripple's monthly escrow unlock (net +300M XRP in August, its tightest ever) vs. ETF demand — when weekly inflows stay under ~$30M, new supply outweighs institutional buying.
  • Whales are accumulating, not selling: large wallets added ~380M XRP in one August week, exchange reserves are down 240M since May, and whale deposits to exchanges are at their lowest since 2021.
  • Regulation is the overhang: the CLARITY Act was shelved before the Senate's August recess — 2026 passage odds fell from ~82% to ~30%. Watch the October window.
  • Demand is global, not just US: in early April a $224M crypto ETP inflow week was led by XRP and was almost entirely European/international — while US funds saw near-zero flows.
  • Underwater cost basis: ~$1.51B has flowed into US ETFs but assets sit near $994M — ETF buyers are collectively ~30% down, a source of overhead sell pressure on rallies.

Exchange outflows & whale activity (August 2026)

  • Exchange reserves shrinking: XRP balances on Upbit, Binance and Bithumb fell ~240M tokens (−2.2%) since late May, to roughly 10.84B XRP combined — source.
  • Outflows go to self-custody: roughly 81% of recent Binance XRP outflows originated from large holders moving coins to private wallets — source.
  • Whales accumulating: 1M–10M XRP wallets added ~380M tokens in one week of August (16.05B → 16.36B) — source; 10M–100M XRP wallets added ~1.23B tokens year-to-date (10.97B → 12.2B) — source.
  • Deposits to exchanges at multi-year lows: whale deposits to Binance hit their lowest level since 2021 (3-month average ≈ $61M) — source.
  • ETF custody: the seven US funds custody ≈ 994.7M XRP (≈ 1% of supply) — source.

Global view — XRP beyond the US

US spot ETFs are the newest and loudest venue, but they are neither the only listed products nor where most XRP actually trades and gets used.

  • Europe (ETPs since 2019–2024): 21Shares' Swiss-listed AXRP (~€213M AUM), WisdomTree's physically-backed XRPW on Xetra/SIX/Euronext (0.50% fee), plus CoinShares products. European/international ETP demand led an early-April $224M crypto inflow week while US funds were near zero.
  • Americas ex-US: Brazil approved the first spot XRP ETF anywhere (Hashdex Nasdaq XRP Fund, XRPH11 on B3); Canadian spot XRP ETFs (Purpose, Evolve, 3iQ) listed in Toronto in mid-2025, ahead of the US.
  • Asia: HashKey Capital launched Asia's first XRP tracker fund in Hong Kong (April 2025, Ripple as anchor investor). Korean won pairs on Upbit/Bithumb are persistently among XRP's largest spot markets — Korean retail, not US institutions, often sets the marginal price.
  • Japan is the real adoption story: the only country using XRP in regulated consumer finance — SBI's ecosystem, FSA-licensed prepaid payment tokens on the XRP Ledger, tokenized bonds with XRP bonuses, and Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin launched via SBI VC Trade (full availability June 2026) — source.
  • Payments corridors: Ripple runs 70+ active ODL corridors (US→Mexico via Bitso, UAE→India via Lulu Exchange, Japan→Thailand via SBI/SCB, Brazil→North America via Travelex) — source.
  • Regulation abroad is ahead of the US: Ripple holds preliminary MiCA CASP approval in Luxembourg and JFSA approvals in Japan — source — while the US CLARITY Act stalls.
